What is KiCad-Astra? KiCad-Astra is a smart assistant that lives inside your KiCad workflow. It helps you design circuit boards faster by suggesting better routes, catching errors early, and getting your files ready for manufacturing — all while keeping you in control. Think of it as having an experienced PCB engineer looking over your shoulder, offering suggestions you can accept or decline.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • 🤖 AI-Assisted Routing: Let the AI propose optimal trace paths that save time and reduce signal issues.
  • 📐 Smarter Layouts: Get placement suggestions for components that improve board balance and performance.
  • 🛡️ Better DRC (Design Rule Check): Catch potential manufacturing problems before your board goes to production.
  • 🏭 Fabrication-Ready Output: Prepare Gerber files and drill marks with confidence, following industry standards.
  • 🔒 Deterministic Local Checks: All critical validation happens on your computer — no cloud dependency, so your designs stay private.
  • ✅ Human Approval Always: You remain the boss. Every AI suggestion can be accepted, modified, or rejected.

🔄 How It Works

  1. Install KiCad-Astra alongside your KiCad software.
  2. Open your PCB design in KiCad.
  3. The AI assistant analyzes your board and provides suggestions marked with a purple glow.
  4. Review each suggestion. Accept, tweak, or ignore them.
  5. Run diagnostics and export fabrication files when you’re happy.

🌟 Key Features Explained

🧩 Smart Component Placement

The AI suggests optimal component positions to minimize track lengths and avoid heat buildup, leading to cleaner boards.

🛤️ Intelligent Router

Helps you route complex nets quickly with automatic bend minimization and clearance checking. Saves hours on dense designs.

🔊 DRC Pre-Scan

Runs a quick scan to identify clearance violations, silkscreen overlaps, or missing footprint errors before your final check.

🏁 One-Click Fabrication

Generate correctly formatted Gerber, drill, and pick-and-place files in one click — right from the board editor.

🔐 Your Privacy, Respected

KiCad-Astra processes everything on your own computer. Your PCB designs never leave your hard drive. That means you’re safe from cloud leaks and intellectual property theft — and you never need an internet connection for the core features.

🛠️ Common Questions & Troubleshooting

  • Is KiCad required? Yes, KiCad-Astra works as a companion plugin for KiCad. You must have KiCad installed first.
  • Does it work on Mac/Linux? The primary supported platform is Windows for now, but the code is Python-based, so future cross-platform support is likely. Check the repository for updates.
  • I see an error about Python missing. KiCad-Astra includes everything it needs, but if you see a message, install the latest Python 3 from python.org and try again.
  • It says "KiCad not found". Please install KiCad from the official website first, then reopen KiCad-Astra.
